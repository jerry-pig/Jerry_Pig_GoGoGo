# 2015 年考研英语（一）Text 2 真题精读与长难句手撕

> **所属模块**：[[00_英语通关导引_MOC]]  
> **文章主题**：传统新闻媒体数字化转型、付费墙与受众消费习惯变革  
> **实战建议**：限时 18 分钟独立闭卷作答 $\to$ 对照解析排查两大诱导陷阱 $\to$ 挑选 2 句长难句脱水

---

## 📄 一、 原文全貌 (Read and Think)

### Paragraph 1
A new survey shows that subscription-based digital news is finally gaining momentum among younger audiences. For decades, traditional print newspapers struggled to convince readers that online content was worth paying for. The prevailing internet culture of "free information" led many media executives to believe that digital advertising was the only viable revenue stream. However, as ad-blockers proliferated and tech giants monopolized digital ad revenue, traditional publishers were forced to rethink their survival strategies.

### Paragraph 2
The transition from advertising dependence to reader-funded models has fundamentally reshaped journalism. Rather than chasing clicks with sensational headlines and cheap viral stories, subscription-driven newsrooms are increasingly incentivized to prioritize editorial depth, investigative reporting, and niche expertise. When readers become direct paying customers, their loyalty depends on credibility and substance rather than fleeting curiosity. Consequently, high-quality journalism is no longer treated as a commodity, but as a specialized service.

### Paragraph 3
Yet this transformation is not without its controversies and societal risks. Critics argue that paywalls create a "two-tier information ecosystem," in which affluent citizens enjoy access to rigorously fact-checked investigative reporting, while less privileged demographics are left to consume low-quality, ad-supported misinformation on social media platforms. In a healthy democracy, equitable access to reliable public information is considered an essential pillar of civic engagement. A media environment segregated by purchasing power may ultimately deepen political polarization.

### Paragraph 4
To address these concerns, several pioneering media organizations are experimenting with hybrid revenue structures. Some offer discounted or subsidized subscriptions for students and low-income households, while others maintain free access to public health emergencies and election coverage. The ultimate sustainability of modern journalism will depend not merely on commercial profitability, but on whether news organizations can balance financial independence with their indispensable public mission.

## 📝 二、 5 道阅读真题演练 (18 分钟限时闭卷)

1. **According to Paragraph 1, traditional publishers originally relied on digital advertising because ___.**  
   [A] print newspapers had lost all their subscribers  
   [B] tech giants refused to cooperate with them  
   [C] readers were reluctant to pay for online content  
   [D] ad-blockers were strictly prohibited by regulations  

2. **The shift toward reader-funded models has encouraged newsrooms to ___.**  
   [A] produce more clickbait and viral stories  
   [B] focus on in-depth and trustworthy reporting  
   [C] reduce the subscription fees for all users  
   [D] treat news content as an ordinary commercial commodity  

3. **The phrase "two-tier information ecosystem" (Line 2, Paragraph 3) implies that ___.**  
   [A] wealthy people are more vulnerable to misinformation  
   [B] social media platforms have replaced traditional journalism  
   [C] reliable news is becoming increasingly divided by wealth  
   [D] digital newsrooms are losing their political influence  

4. **According to critics, paywalls might harm democracy because they ___.**  
   [A] restrict the public's equal access to reliable information  
   [B] discourage civic participation among affluent demographics  
   [C] lower the standard of investigative journalism  
   [D] increase government censorship on private media  

5. **The author concludes that the long-term future of modern journalism depends on ___.**  
   [A] eliminating all commercial advertising from digital platforms  
   [B] balancing commercial viability with social responsibility  
   [C] relying entirely on government subsidies and donations  
   [D] forcing social media companies to pay for news content

## 🔑 三、 标准答案与黄金排雷解析

- **标准答案**：**`1-C, 2-B, 3-C, 4-A, 5-B`**

### 1. 第 1 题解析 (事实细节题)
- **定位**：Para. 1, Line 2-3 *"The prevailing internet culture of 'free information' led many media executives to believe..."*
- **同义替换**：`reluctant to pay` 替换了原文的 `struggled to convince readers that online content was worth paying for`。
- **干扰项排查**：A 选项“失去了所有订阅者”属于【过度绝对化】；D 选项“广告拦截被法律禁止”属于【正反颠倒】。

### 2. 第 2 题解析 (事实细节题)
- **定位**：Para. 2, Line 2 *"subscription-driven newsrooms are increasingly incentivized to prioritize editorial depth..."*
- **同义替换**：`in-depth and trustworthy reporting` 替换了原文的 `editorial depth, investigative reporting, and niche expertise`。

### 3. 第 3 题解析 (词义推断题)
- **定位**：Para. 3, Line 2-4 *"affluent citizens enjoy access... while less privileged demographics are left to consume low-quality..."*
- **逻辑推断**：富人能看高质量事实核查报道，穷人只能看社交媒体劣质假新闻，说明可靠信息因财富产生鸿沟，对应 C `reliable news is becoming increasingly divided by wealth`。

### 4. 第 4 题解析 (事实细节题)
- **定位**：Para. 3, Line 5-6 *"In a healthy democracy, equitable access to reliable public information is considered an essential pillar..."*
- **同义替换**：`restrict the public's equal access` 对应原文 `equitable access` 被破坏。

### 5. 第 5 题解析 (主旨结论题)
- **定位**：Para. 4 最后一句 *"balance financial independence with their indispensable public mission."*
- **同义替换**：`balance commercial viability with social responsibility` 完美改写了财务独立与公共使命的平衡。

## 🔪 四、 长难句“三刀切”极简脱水手撕示范

### 难句 1（第三段第 2 句）：
> *"Critics argue that paywalls create a 'two-tier information ecosystem,' in which affluent citizens enjoy access to rigorously fact-checked investigative reporting, while less privileged demographics are left to consume low-quality, ad-supported misinformation on social media platforms."*

- **[第一刀：剥除修饰]**：
  - 宾语从句：`that paywalls create a 'two-tier information ecosystem'`
  - 介词+which定语从句：`(in which affluent citizens enjoy access to ...)`
  - 对比状语从句：`[while less privileged demographics are left to consume ... (on social media platforms)]`
- **[第二刀：提取主干 (SVO)]**：
  - **Critics** (主) + **argue** (谓) + **that-clause** (宾).
  - 从句主干：**Paywalls** (从主) + **create** (从谓) + **ecosystem** (从宾).
- **[第三刀：流畅顺译]**：
  > 批评人士指出，付费墙制造了一种“两层分化的信息生态系统”：富裕公民享有经过严格事实核查的深度调查报道，而低收入阶层则只能在社交媒体上消费充斥着广告的劣质虚假信息。

## 💎 五、 今日高频语境词汇库 (Lexicon)

1. **proliferate** `/prəˈlɪfəreɪt/` *vi.* 激增、扩散（pro-向前 + life生命 $\to$ 迅速繁殖增生）
2. **viable** /ˈvaɪəbl/ *adj.* 可行的、能存活的（viable revenue stream 可行的收入来源）
3. **affluent** /ˈæfluənt/ *adj.* 富裕的、富足的（af-向 + flu流动 $\to$ 钱财流入口袋）
4. **equitable** /ˈekwɪtəbl/ *adj.* 公平的、公正合理的（equal衍生词）
5. **polarization** /ˌpoʊləraɪˈzeɪʃn/ *n.* 两极分化（polar极点 + -ization化）
