# 2015 年考研英语（一）Text 1 深度精读与长难句手撕

> **文章主题**：欧洲君主制的衰落与现代王室的平民化（社会与政治文化）  
> **实战定位**：考研英语（一）经典真题第一篇，非常适合作为**弱项破局的第一场实战精读**！  
> **下午目标**：18 分钟限时做题 $\to$ 手撕 2 句长难句主干 $\to$ 提炼 5 个高频词 $\to$ 搞懂干扰项套路。

---

## 📄 一、 原文全貌 (Read and Think)

**Paragraph 1**  
King Juan Carlos of Spain once casually remarked? "Monarchs ought to be respected, not praised." But like other public figures, monarchs are easily tempted by applause and popularity. Over the past twenty years, Queen Elizabeth II has promoted the family brand with the marketing genius of an American corporation. Prince Charles, an heir in waiting, has championed causes as diverse as organic farming, urban planning, and architecture. Meanwhile, the Scandinavian royal families have adopted a more low-profile, bicycle-riding style.

**Paragraph 2**  
Yet the real danger for European monarchs lies not in their public activities, but in the rising expectations of their citizens. In an era dominated by democratic ideals and financial transparency, hereditary privilege appears increasingly anachronistic. Citizens demand that royal spending be subjected to strict parliamentary scrutiny. The public no longer tolerates scandals, excessive lavishness, or special tax exemptions that were once granted without question.

**Paragraph 3**  
To preserve their legitimacy, many royal houses have initiated sweeping reforms. They have drastically reduced the civil list—the taxpayer-funded allowance for working royals—and volunteered to pay income taxes on their private estates. In Britain, Prince William and Prince Harry have sought to cultivate an image of approachable, modern young men who are dedicated to mental health awareness and military veterans, rather than distant aristocratic figures.

**Paragraph 4**  
However, critics argue that these attempts at modernization are fundamentally contradictory. A monarchy is, by definition, an institution rooted in ancient hierarchy and hereditary inequality. If royals try too hard to be "just like us," they risk undermining the very mystique that justifies their constitutional existence. As the 19th-century political theorist Walter Bagehot famously warned, the monarchy’s survival depends on maintaining a sense of magic and mystery: "We must not let in daylight upon magic."

## 📝 二、 5 道阅读真题演练 (18 分钟限时闭卷)

## ⚔️ 三、 2 大核心长难句手撕剥离 (Syntax Analysis)

### 🔴 难句 1（主谓被长定语拉开）
> *"The public no longer tolerates scandals, excessive lavishness, or special tax exemptions that were once granted without question."*

- **【第 1 步：剥离主谓宾骨架】**
  - **主语**：The public（公众）
  - **谓语**：no longer tolerates（不再容忍）
  - **并列宾语**：scandals（丑闻）, excessive lavishness（过度奢华）, or special tax exemptions（特殊免税特权）。
- **【第 2 步：剥离修饰从句】**
  - `that were once granted without question`（定语从句，修饰前面的三种特权）。
- **【参考译文】**：
  > 公众不再容忍那些曾经被毫无疑问地赋予他们的丑闻、过度奢华以及特殊的免税特权。

---

### 🔴 难句 2（条件状语从句 + 宾语从句嵌套）
> *"If royals try too hard to be 'just like us,' they risk undermining the very mystique that justifies their constitutional existence."*

- **【第 1 步：剥离骨架】**
  - **主干**：they risk undermining the very mystique（他们冒着削弱那份神秘感的风险）。
- **【第 2 步：括号修饰成分】**
  - `(If royals try too hard to be "just like us")`（条件状语从句：如果王室过于努力地表现得“和我们普通人一样”）；
  - `[that justifies their constitutional existence]`（that 引导定语从句，修饰 mystique：证明他们在宪法上存在的合法性的）。
- **【核心生词】**：*undermine*（削弱、破坏）；*mystique*（神秘色彩）；*justify*（证明……具有正当性）。
- **【参考译文】**：
  > 如果王室成员过于努力地表现得“和我们普通人一模一样”，他们就会冒着削弱那份赋予其宪法存在正当性的神秘色彩的风险。

## 🔑 四、 参考答案与干扰项防坑解析

1. **[B] 追求大众吸引力**（首段提到 monarchs are easily tempted by applause and popularity，B是同义替换）。
2. **[C] 过时的、不合时宜的**（ana- 错位 + chron 时间，即“与时代脱节”）。
3. **[C] 削减公共财政支持**（第三段 drastically reduced the civil list = taxpayer-funded allowance，C为精准同义替换）。
4. **[A] 破坏其宪政神秘感**（第四段 risk undermining the very mystique 直接对应）。
5. **[B] 保持一定程度的神秘感**（"We must not let in daylight upon magic" 即不能让阳光照进魔法，B项保持秘密）。
