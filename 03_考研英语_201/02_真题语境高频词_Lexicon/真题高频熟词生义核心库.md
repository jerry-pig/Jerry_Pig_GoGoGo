## 🎯 高频致命熟词生义 30 弹 (Top Lethal Words)

| 12 | **check** | 检查、支票 | *vt./n.* **抑制、制止、制约** | checks and balances (权力制衡) / check inflation |

| 20 | **harbor** | 港口海港 | *vt.* **心怀、藏匿（恶意/疑虑）** | harbor resentment / harbor doubts |
| 21 | **industry** | 工业、行业 | *n.* **勤奋、勤勉** | reward one's industry / sheer industry |
| 22 | **observe** | 观察 | *vt.* **遵守（法律/规则）；评述** | observe the regulations / as observed earlier |

| 26 | **relief** | 救济、宽慰 | *n.* **鲜明突出、浮雕** | in sharp relief (格外鲜明突出) |
| 27 | **secure** | 安全的 | *vt.* **获得、锁定、争取到** | secure a loan / secure a contract |
